# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Dream Customs serves people who wake up with vivid, strange, or unsettling dreams and want a small morning ritual before the day takes over. The primary user may still be half asleep, on a phone, and emotionally sticky from the dream. The secondary user is a hackathon judge or playful AI explorer who needs to understand the loop quickly and see why small multimodal models matter.

## Product Purpose

Dream Customs helps a user form a playful alliance with last night's dream. It accepts text, image, and voice evidence, turns those fragments into a structured dream declaration, asks a few customs questions, and lets the user choose whether to add more material, continue negotiation, or seal a next-day pact.

Success means the user receives something more useful than a one-shot interpretation: a clear visitor, a practical suggestion for today, a weird five-minute task, and a bedtime release phrase. The experience should feel like a small ceremony, not a diagnosis.

## Brand Personality

Gentle, strange, lucid.

The voice is an overnight border clerk who takes odd evidence seriously without becoming mystical or clinical. It should feel curious and companionable, with enough dream color to be memorable and enough product discipline to be usable on a phone.

## Anti-references

- Do not look like a plain Gradio form with two columns and one submit button.
- Do not look like a beige parchment certificate, generic tarot card, therapy intake form, or purple AI SaaS dashboard.
- Do not use medical, diagnostic, frightening, or fate-telling language.
- Do not bury the main action below many controls.
- Do not end the flow after one answer when the user still wants to add evidence or keep negotiating.

## Design Principles

1. The AI is a clerk in a ritual, not a text generator behind a form.
2. The user keeps agency: add evidence, answer, skip, continue, revise, or seal.
3. The interface should be dream-colored but product-trustworthy.
4. Every output should lead to a tiny next-day action.
5. The demo path must survive Space constraints and still have a model-backed route.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for body text, controls, and placeholder text. Support mobile-first use, keyboard navigation, visible focus states, reduced motion, and Chinese/English text without layout breakage. Avoid flashing effects, fear-based copy, or overconfident claims about dream meaning. Severe distress, self-harm, harm to others, or inability to function must show a clear support note.
